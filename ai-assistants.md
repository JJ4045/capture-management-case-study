# FAR and Sales Strategy Assistants

**Joseph Johnson | SRS portfolio | Project overview**

I created a FAR bot using GPT and a sales bot for the company. These tools extend my work in business support, government contracting, and AI coaching.

## FAR assistant

**Purpose:** Help new businesses understand the Federal Acquisition Regulation through questions and answers. I described the tool as a live-action assistant for navigating the rules.

**My contribution:** Created the GPT-based assistant for this business-learning use case.

**Portfolio scope:** This summary documents the purpose and creation of the tool. It does not establish live retrieval, automatic regulatory updates, legal accuracy, adoption levels, or evaluated performance. Public access and demonstration materials have not been supplied.

## Sales strategy assistant

**Purpose:** Help company users maintain focus while brainstorming sales approaches or reviewing current sales strategies.

**My contribution:** Created the company sales bot for strategy brainstorming and review.

**Portfolio scope:** This overview does not claim autonomous outreach, CRM integration, conversion improvements, or revenue attribution. Those capabilities and results have not been established here.

## Proposed demonstration and review plan

The following are proposed next steps, not tests already completed:

| Tool | Demonstration | Review criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FAR assistant | Ask a basic question about teaming, then one that depends on solicitation or agency context | Verify citations against current official text; distinguish general explanation from transaction-specific interpretation; surface uncertainty |
| FAR assistant | Ask about a change in regulations and provide an ambiguous fact pattern | Confirm it does not invent updates or confidently answer without sufficient context; document human verification |
| Sales strategy assistant | Give a fictional small-business sales scenario and ask for a strategy critique | Check whether suggestions address the stated customer, capacity, constraints, and next steps |
| Sales strategy assistant | Ask it to review an unsupported revenue claim | Check whether it flags missing evidence and avoids inventing customer or performance data |

Use fictional or authorized sanitized inputs for demonstrations. Record the date, configuration, prompt, response, reviewer corrections, and outcome. Do not expose confidential company material, procurement-sensitive information, or PHI in a public demonstration.
