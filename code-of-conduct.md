# Teaming Cooperative Code of Conduct

**Portfolio example | Prepared by Joseph Johnson | SRS consulting | October 15, 2026**

This proposed code gives cooperative members a common basis for trust, confidentiality, fair participation, and accountable opportunity pursuit. It adapts themes from my earlier chapter ethics and member-rights publications. It is a newly prepared portfolio example, not a claim that this version was formally adopted.

## Scope and member commitments

Members, facilitators, advisers, and invited contributors should follow this code during cooperative meetings, CRM use, outreach, and jointly authorized pursuits. Each company retains its independent business judgment.

1. **Communicate accurately.** Represent capabilities, credentials, past performance, availability, and commitments honestly. Attribute another member's work accurately and obtain permission before using its name, logo, or references.
2. **Protect confidential information.** Share only what is authorized and needed for the specific pursuit. Agree on confidentiality terms before exchanging sensitive information. Use approved channels and access restrictions; do not upload proprietary material to AI services without authorization and suitable protections.
3. **Disclose conflicts.** Promptly identify competing commitments, personal interests, and potential organizational conflicts to the designated facilitator or compliance contact. Pause affected work until the responsible authority determines the response.
4. **Preserve procurement integrity and competition.** Do not seek or share improperly obtained bid, proposal, or source-selection information. Do not coordinate independent bids, allocate markets, or exchange unrelated competitive pricing. Refer questionable exchanges to compliance or counsel.
5. **Make teaming expectations explicit.** Agree on pursuit roles, decision authority, contribution deadlines, attribution, and proposed work allocation in writing. Allocation should reflect negotiated responsibilities; membership alone does not promise work, profits, exclusivity, or an award.
6. **Respect intellectual property.** Ownership remains with the owner unless a separate written agreement states otherwise. Obtain permission for reuse, distribution, or incorporation into a proposal.
7. **Participate constructively.** Attend or provide updates for weekly meetings, listen respectfully, disclose capacity limits, and notify the team early when commitments may slip. Avoid pressure, harassment, exclusion, and retaliatory conduct.
8. **Record pursuit decisions.** Discuss the win strategy before writing begins. Log bid/no-bid decisions, authorized decision-makers, open conditions, next steps, owners, and due dates. Revisit the decision when material facts change.
9. **Respect member participation.** Provide a fair opportunity to raise concerns, offer relevant capabilities, obtain agreed updates, and develop skills. Respect differing views and apply objective opportunity criteria.
10. **Report concerns and learn.** Raise suspected misconduct promptly. Cooperate with a fair review, preserve relevant records, and carry out agreed corrective actions.

## Concerns and fair review

Report concerns to **[facilitator or ethics contact]** through **[private reporting channel]**. If that person is involved, contact **[alternate reviewer]**. Limit disclosure to people needed for a fair review; explain that absolute confidentiality cannot be promised where reporting or disclosure is legally required.

The reviewer acknowledges the concern, identifies conflicts in the review process, gathers relevant facts, gives affected members a reasonable opportunity to respond, and documents the decision. Protect good-faith reporting and participation from retaliation. Refer suspected unlawful conduct to the appropriate compliance or legal authority.

Possible responses include clarification, coaching, corrective action, limited access, pursuit reassignment, or suspension or termination of membership under the cooperative's governing agreement. Provide a documented review or appeal route through **[independent contact]**. This code does not itself create authority to override a signed agreement or applicable law.
