# Example HIPAA Business Associate Agreement

**Unsigned portfolio example | October 15, 2026 | Legal review required before use**

This example addresses a covered entity engaging a business associate for services involving protected health information (PHI). It is not an executed agreement or a compliance certification. Counsel must confirm the relationship, services, current law, and all blanks before use. A general teaming relationship alone does not establish a HIPAA business associate relationship.

Terms including PHI, electronic PHI, Breach, Security Incident, Individual, Subcontractor, and Secretary have their applicable meanings under the HIPAA Rules at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164. Duties below apply to PHI created, received, maintained, or transmitted on the Covered Entity's behalf.

## Permitted use

The Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only for the authorized services, this agreement, or a legal requirement. Apply the minimum necessary standard where applicable. No independent marketing, sale, model training, product development, or unrelated analytics is authorized. Additional permissions require lawful written terms; authorization by a contract cannot override HIPAA.

## Safeguards and downstream recipients

The Business Associate will implement appropriate safeguards and comply with the applicable HIPAA Security Rule requirements for electronic PHI. Restrict access to authorized personnel and approved systems. Do not enter PHI into an AI service or other external tool without prior written approval and all required contractual and technical protections.

Before a subcontractor handles PHI on its behalf, the Business Associate will obtain a written agreement imposing the applicable restrictions, conditions, and Security Rule duties. Maintain a record of authorized downstream providers.

## Incidents and breaches

Report unauthorized uses or disclosures and security incidents to the Covered Entity. For this example, the parties propose initial notice within **24 hours of discovery**, followed by available details and regular updates. This is a proposed contractual deadline, not a statement of HIPAA's universal deadline.

For a breach of unsecured PHI, provide notice without unreasonable delay and no later than the applicable limit in 45 CFR 164.410, including affected individuals and available information needed for required notifications. Do not delay notice until investigation is complete. Preserve evidence and cooperate in containment, risk assessment, remediation, and notification duties. Allocation of external notices must be agreed in writing and comply with law.

## Individual rights and oversight

For designated record set information, provide access assistance and PHI needed for the Covered Entity's individual-access obligations; make and incorporate amendments as directed; and maintain disclosure information needed for an accounting. Agree on response deadlines that allow the Covered Entity to meet its legal deadlines. Forward requests received directly to the designated contact promptly.

When performing a Covered Entity obligation under the Privacy Rule, comply with the requirements applicable to that obligation. Make relevant practices, books, and records available to the Secretary for HIPAA oversight.

## Covered Entity responsibilities

Communicate applicable privacy-notice limitations, authorization changes, and restrictions affecting the services. Do not request a use or disclosure that would violate HIPAA if performed by the Covered Entity, except where a lawful exception applies. Supply only authorized information through approved channels.

## Termination and retention

This agreement continues through **[termination event]**, subject to surviving duties. The Covered Entity may terminate for a material violation; any cure opportunity must be specified and legally appropriate. At termination, return or destroy PHI where feasible and retain no copies. If infeasible, document the reason, extend protection, and limit further use or disclosure to the reason retention is necessary for as long as retained. Require corresponding downstream handling.

## Completion before execution

Complete service scope, systems, contacts, incident procedures, individual-rights response periods, termination terms, and signatures. Counsel must address governing law, notices, remedies, insurance, liability, state law, and interactions with the services agreement. Amend as needed for applicable law; interpret ambiguities consistently with HIPAA.
