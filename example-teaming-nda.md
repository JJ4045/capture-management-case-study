# Example Mutual Teaming Nondisclosure Agreement

**Portfolio example | October 15, 2026 | For legal review before business use**

This unsigned example protects information exchanged to evaluate a specific teaming opportunity. It is newly drafted for the portfolio and is not an executed client agreement. Complete the blanks and have qualified counsel adapt the terms to the parties, jurisdiction, and acquisition before use.

## Parties and purpose

**[Full legal name and address of Party A]** and **[Full legal name and address of Party B]** agree, effective **[date]**, to exchange information solely to evaluate and, if separately agreed in writing, prepare a joint pursuit of **[opportunity, agency, and solicitation reference]** (the Purpose). Each may act as a disclosing or receiving party. Additional cooperative members must join through a written accession or separate agreement before receiving protected information.

## Confidential information

Confidential Information means nonpublic business, technical, pricing, customer, personnel, capture, or proposal information disclosed for the Purpose and identified as confidential, or reasonably understood to be confidential from its nature and context. Confirm oral disclosures in writing within **[agreed period]** to reduce ambiguity. Notes and derivatives revealing the information receive the same protection.

Information is excluded to the extent the recipient can demonstrate that it was lawfully known without a duty of confidence, becomes public without breach, is independently developed without use of the information, or is lawfully received from a third party without a confidentiality duty.

## Use and access restrictions

The recipient will use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and protect it with reasonable care, at least equal to the care it uses for comparable sensitive information. Limit access to personnel and advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by duties at least as protective. The recipient is responsible for their compliance to the extent permitted by law. Do not disclose to other cooperative members, subcontractors, affiliates, or vendors without authorization and suitable obligations.

Do not copy more than needed, remove ownership markings, or use the information to disadvantage the owner outside the Purpose. Use agreed storage and transfer channels. Do not submit the information to a public or third-party AI tool without the discloser's written permission and agreed data controls.

## Protected and regulated information

This agreement does not authorize disclosure of classified information, controlled unclassified information, export-controlled data, personal health information, or procurement information that a party is not entitled to disclose. Establish separate authority and required handling arrangements before any such exchange. Do not accept improperly obtained competitor or source-selection information.

## Required disclosures and protected reporting

If disclosure is legally required, give prompt notice when lawful, cooperate reasonably in seeking protection, and disclose only what is legally required. Nothing prohibits lawful reporting to regulators, law enforcement, inspectors general, or other protected whistleblower disclosures, or requires advance permission for those reports. Counsel should assess any additional notices required for the intended recipients and jurisdiction.

## Ownership and relationship

The discloser retains its rights. No intellectual-property license is granted beyond limited use for the Purpose. No party is obligated to disclose information or enter a teaming agreement, subcontract, joint venture, exclusivity arrangement, or bid. No workshare, award, revenue, or performance guarantee is created. Agree separately on proposal submission permissions, workshare, roles, and any exclusivity.

Information is supplied for evaluation without a warranty of accuracy or completeness, except as separately agreed; this provision does not excuse fraud or misconduct. Each party bears its own evaluation costs unless agreed otherwise.

## Incidents and return or destruction

Notify the discloser promptly upon discovering suspected unauthorized access, use, or disclosure; preserve evidence, contain the issue, and cooperate in mitigation. Agree on reporting contacts and a specific reporting timeframe before exchange.

On request or termination, stop use and return or securely destroy Confidential Information and derivatives, confirming completion on request. A legally required archival copy and inaccessible routine backups may be retained only under continuing protection, with no further use except required compliance and orderly backup deletion.

## Term and enforcement

The exchange period is **[start and end dates]**. Either party may end further exchange by written notice. Confidentiality and use restrictions continue for **[negotiated duration]** after each disclosure; trade secrets remain protected while legally qualifying as trade secrets. Accrued rights and agreed retention duties survive.

The parties will first escalate disputes to designated executives, without preventing urgent lawful relief. Available remedies, including any injunctive relief, remain subject to applicable law and court discretion. Governing law, forum, notice addresses, liability allocation, and any dispute process must be completed as **[negotiated terms]**. Amendments and waivers require authorized written agreement. An invalid provision does not invalidate the remainder to the extent permitted by law.
