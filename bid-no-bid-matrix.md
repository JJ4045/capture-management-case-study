# Basic Bid or No Bid Decision Matrix

**Portfolio example | Joseph Johnson | October 15, 2026**

Use this proposed worksheet to make a documented pursuit decision before committing proposal resources. The weights and thresholds below are illustrative management choices, not a FAR requirement or a reconstruction of a client's scoring system. A score supports judgment; it does not authorize a bid or estimate probability of winning.

## Step one Check mandatory gates

Record **Yes**, **No**, or **Unknown**, with evidence and a responsible owner. A confirmed disqualifier gives **No Bid** for the current configuration. An unresolved gate gives **Hold**, with an owner and a deadline before proposal commitment. Reassess only when the gap is resolved or the configuration changes lawfully. An authorized decision-maker cannot waive a legal or solicitation requirement.

| Gate | Evidence to record | Status | Owner and due date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible to compete in the intended role | Applicable solicitation, vehicle access, registration, set-aside status, and exclusions review | [Y/N/U] | [complete] |
| Mandatory requirements can be met by required dates | Required qualifications, licenses, clearances, delivery, and staffing | [Y/N/U] | [complete] |
| Procurement integrity and conflicts addressed | Conflict review, information provenance, mitigation or approval where required | [Y/N/U] | [complete] |
| Authorized access and protection of sensitive information | NDA, handling plan, required permissions and security capabilities | [Y/N/U] | [complete] |
| Resources and schedule are feasible | Proposal budget, accountable staff, realistic review and submission plan | [Y/N/U] | [complete] |
| Required teammates committed | Named roles, capacity, authorized commitment, and necessary agreements | [Y/N/U] | [complete] |

For a cooperative pursuit intended to involve two or more members, specifically confirm at least two committed members and complementary roles. This is a proposed cooperative operating rule, not a universal acquisition requirement.

## Step two Score only after all gates pass

Rate each factor from **0 to 5**. Use 0 for no demonstrated basis, 1 for weak, 2 for limited, 3 for adequate, 4 for strong, and 5 for compelling, evidenced fit. Record Unknown separately; do not substitute a guessed rating. Any Unknown keeps the scoring recommendation on Hold until resolved.

| Factor | Weight | Evidence question | Rating 0 to 5 |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Customer and requirement understanding | 20 | Do we understand the stated need and evaluation criteria through lawful evidence? | [ ] |
| Win strategy and differentiation | 20 | Can we explain why this team should be selected and support that position? | [ ] |
| Technical and delivery fit | 15 | Can we demonstrate the skills, resources, and delivery approach? | [ ] |
| Relevant past performance | 15 | Is the evidence relevant and usable under the solicitation? | [ ] |
| Team readiness and complementary roles | 10 | Are contributions, capacity, decision authority, and commitments clear? | [ ] |
| Financial feasibility | 10 | Are expected costs, cash needs, and pricing assumptions supportable? | [ ] |
| Proposal execution readiness | 10 | Can we produce a compliant, reviewed submission on time? | [ ] |
| **Total** | **100** | | |

**Weighted points for a factor = weight × rating ÷ 5.** Add the seven point values for a total from 0 to 100. Weights must total 100; verify ratings are within 0–5 and every factor has evidence. A decimal rating is permitted only if the team agrees on that convention in advance.

## Step three Record the recommendation and decision

- **80–100:** Recommend Bid, subject to documented approval and an agreed win strategy.
- **60–less than 80:** Hold for a targeted improvement plan and another decision before the commitment deadline.
- **Below 60:** Recommend No Bid.
- **Any failed or unknown gate, or unknown rating:** Apply the gate and unknown rules before interpreting the total.

An authorized decision-maker records **Bid**, **No Bid**, or **Hold**, the rationale, conditions, owner, and next review date. If the decision differs from the scoring recommendation, document why. Legal and eligibility gates remain mandatory. If a Hold cannot be resolved before the commitment deadline, record No Bid for the current pursuit.

## Weekly meeting and CRM follow-through

Record the opportunity, decision, evidence, win strategy, member roles, actions, owners, and due dates in the approved CRM. Use weekly meetings to review commitments and changes in facts. Escalate missed actions before they affect the proposal. Revisit the decision when the solicitation, partners, staffing, risk, or economics materially change.
